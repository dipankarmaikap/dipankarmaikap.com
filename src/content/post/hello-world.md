I’m excited to finally launch my blog! I’ve been thinking about starting one for a while, and it’s finally happening. To be honest, I’m both nervous and excited! 😀

## My Story

I started my coding journey in 2018. My first experience with code was when I had to replace a few URLs in an HTML email template for a client. Shortly after, they asked me to change the background color of an element. A quick Google search—“how to change the background color of an element”—led me deeper into the world of coding. Since then, I’ve fallen in love with web development.

I taught myself HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and WordPress. Over the past year, I’ve been learning React.js and Node.js to expand my skill set.

## The Purpose of This Blog

As a self-taught web developer, I’ve spent countless hours searching for answers across multiple forums and websites. This blog is my way of giving back by sharing what I’ve learned. My goals are to:

- Share my journey as a web developer.
- Explain day-to-day development challenges and their solutions.
- Learn new technologies and document my experiences.
- Improve my English writing skills along the way.

## Have a Question?

I’m not an expert—I’m learning new things every day. If there’s a topic you’d like me to cover, feel free to email me, and I’ll do my best to create a post about it.

Thank you for reading! I hope my posts help you learn something new.

Take care! 🙂
